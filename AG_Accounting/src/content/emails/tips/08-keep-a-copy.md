Once your return is filed, save a complete copy with the documents that support it.

You’ll likely need it to:

- Prepare next year’s return
- Apply for a mortgage or student aid
- Answer an IRS or state letter
- Amend the return if something changes

In most cases, keep returns and supporting records for at least three years after filing. Keep records about property, like a home or investments, until three years after you report its sale. Many people simply keep copies of their returns permanently.
