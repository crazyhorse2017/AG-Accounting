Your filing status sets your tax brackets and standard deduction, and it can change from year to year. Your status on December 31 generally applies to the whole year.

Take a second look if, during the year, you:

- Got married or divorced
- Had a baby or a dependent moved in or out
- Became a single parent paying most of the household costs (you may qualify as head of household)
- Lost a spouse (you may qualify as a surviving spouse for two more years)

Head of household, in particular, is often missed. For 2026 it brings a $24,150 standard deduction instead of $16,100 for single filers.
