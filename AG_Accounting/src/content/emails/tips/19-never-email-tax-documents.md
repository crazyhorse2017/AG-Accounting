Tax returns, W-2s, and anything with a Social Security number or bank account number shouldn’t be sent by regular email. Email can be intercepted or end up in the wrong inbox.

To share documents with us:

- Use our secure client portal, or
- Bring them to our office.

We will never ask you for your Social Security number, bank account number or card number by email or text. If you ever get a message that claims to be from us and asks for that kind of information, call our office before responding.
