Most Ohio cities have an income tax. Your employer usually withholds it for the city where you work, but your home city may also tax your income.

Most home cities give a credit for tax paid to your work city, but credits differ. When the credit is less than your home city’s tax, you owe the difference, and no one may be withholding it for you.

Check your W-2 boxes 18 through 20 to see which cities had tax withheld. Our [city income tax calculator](/calculators/ohio-municipal-income-tax) gives a quick estimate.
