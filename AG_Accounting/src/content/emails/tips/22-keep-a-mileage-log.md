If you use your car for business, a mileage log is the key to your deduction. For each business trip, record the date, destination, business purpose, and miles. Write down your odometer reading at the start and end of the year, too.

A notebook in the glove box or a phone app both work, as long as you record trips at or near the time you take them.

Remember: commuting between home and your regular workplace isn’t deductible. Driving between business stops, to clients, or to pick up supplies usually is.
