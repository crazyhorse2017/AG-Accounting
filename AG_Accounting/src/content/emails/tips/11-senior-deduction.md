From 2025 through 2028, people 65 and older may deduct an extra $6,000 each, or $12,000 for a married couple if both qualify. It phases out for modified adjusted gross income over $75,000 ($150,000 for joint filers).

This is on top of the existing additional standard deduction for seniors, which for 2026 is $2,050 if you’re unmarried or $1,650 per qualifying spouse if married.

You can take the new deduction whether or not you itemize. If you’re 65 or older, make sure your preparer knows your birth date.
