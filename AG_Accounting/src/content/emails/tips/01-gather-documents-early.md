Tax forms start arriving in January, and they’re easy to lose in a pile of mail. Start one folder, paper or digital, and drop every tax document into it the day it arrives.

What to look for:

- W-2s from every job
- 1099 forms for contract work, interest, dividends, and retirement income
- Form 1098 for mortgage interest
- Charity receipts and child care records

When the folder looks complete, use our [appointment checklist](/tax-center/appointment-checklist) to see what’s missing. A complete folder means a faster, more accurate return.
