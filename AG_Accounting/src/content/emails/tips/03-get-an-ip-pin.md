An Identity Protection PIN is a six-digit number known only to you and the IRS. Once you have one, any return filed with your Social Security number must include it, which blocks identity thieves from filing a fake return and taking your refund.

Anyone with a Social Security number or ITIN who can verify their identity can get one. The fastest way is the IRS’s online [Get an IP PIN tool](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin).

You get a new PIN every year, so bring the current one to your tax appointment. And never share it with anyone except your tax preparer.
