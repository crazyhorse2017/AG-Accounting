Unlike 401(k) contributions, IRA contributions for 2026 can be made right up until the filing deadline, April 15, 2027.

For 2026, you can contribute up to $7,500, or $8,600 if you’re 50 or older.

A traditional IRA contribution may be deductible, which can lower last year’s tax bill. A Roth IRA contribution isn’t deductible, but qualified withdrawals later are tax-free.

When you contribute between January 1 and April 15, make sure your IRA provider records it for the correct year.
