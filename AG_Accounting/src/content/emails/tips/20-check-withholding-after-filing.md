If you owed a large amount this year, or got a very large refund, your withholding is probably off.

The IRS [Tax Withholding Estimator](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator) takes about 15 minutes. Have your recent pay stubs and the return you just filed handy. It will suggest changes to your Form W-4, which you can give your employer any time.

Doing this in spring gives you most of the year to even things out, instead of trying to catch up in December.
