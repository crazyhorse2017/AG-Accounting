Employers must give you your W-2 by January 31, or the next business day. In 2027, January 31 falls on a Sunday, so the deadline is Monday, February 1. Many 1099 forms are due the same day.

If one hasn’t arrived by mid-February:

1. Check your online payroll or account portal; many forms are available there.
2. Ask the employer or payer for a copy.
3. Confirm they have your correct mailing address.

Don’t file using a final pay stub instead of the W-2. The numbers often differ, and a mismatch can lead to an IRS letter later.
