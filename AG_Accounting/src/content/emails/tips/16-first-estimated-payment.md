If you’re self-employed, a landlord, or have other income without withholding, your first 2027 estimated tax payment is due April 15, the same day as your 2026 return.

A simple way to figure it: take last year’s total tax, divide by four, and pay that each quarter. If last year’s adjusted gross income was over $150,000, use 110% of last year’s tax instead.
