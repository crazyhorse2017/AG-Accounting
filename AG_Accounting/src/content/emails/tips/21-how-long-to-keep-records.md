Now that filing season is over, it’s a good time to clean out old papers. The general rule is to keep records that support your return for **three years** after you file.

Keep them longer if:

- You left income off a return that was more than 25% of the income shown: six years
- You claimed a loss on worthless securities or a bad debt: seven years
- You have employees: keep employment tax records at least four years
- The records relate to property you still own: until three years after you sell it

Shred anything with personal information.
