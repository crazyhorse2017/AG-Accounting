The second 2027 estimated tax payment is due June 15. It covers income earned in April and May.

If your income has changed a lot since April, now is a good time to adjust. Paying too little can lead to an underpayment penalty; paying far too much ties up money you could use in your business.

You can pay federal estimates through IRS Direct Pay at no cost. Don’t forget your Ohio and city estimates if you make them.
