If you’re expecting a refund, the fastest route is to file electronically and choose direct deposit. The IRS issues most refunds for e-filed returns in less than 21 days.

A few tips:

- Double-check your bank routing and account numbers. A typo can delay your money for weeks.
- The account should be in your name.
- By law, refunds that include the Earned Income Tax Credit or Additional Child Tax Credit can’t be issued before mid-February.

You can track your refund with the IRS’s [Where’s My Refund?](https://www.irs.gov/refunds) tool, which updates once a day.
