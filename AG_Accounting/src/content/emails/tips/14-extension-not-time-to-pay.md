Form 4868 gives you an automatic six-month extension to file your return, to October 15. But it doesn’t give you more time to pay.

If you’ll owe, estimate the amount and pay it by the April deadline. Otherwise, interest and a late-payment penalty start to build.

An extension is useful when you’re waiting on a form or need more time to get records together. Ohio and most cities have their own extension rules, so check those too.
