If you paid for child care so you could work or look for work, you may qualify for the child and dependent care credit. For 2026, qualifying expenses are capped at $3,000 for one person and $6,000 for two or more.

To claim it, you must report the care provider’s:

- Name
- Address
- Taxpayer identification number (an SSN or EIN)

Ask your provider for this now, before you’re waiting on it in April. Day camp can count; overnight camp doesn’t.
