title: Don’t file too early

It’s tempting to file the moment your W-2 arrives, especially if you’re expecting a refund. But some forms, like brokerage 1099s and Schedule K-1s, often arrive later.

If you file and then receive a form that changes your return, you’ll need to file an amended return, Form 1040-X, and the IRS may send a letter about the missing income.

Before you file, ask yourself:

- Did I have any investment accounts?
- Did I do any contract or gig work?
- Do I own part of a partnership or S corporation?

If so, wait until those forms arrive.
