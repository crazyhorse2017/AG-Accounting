Many tax changes start with a life change. Let us know if this year you:

- Got married, divorced, or widowed
- Had or adopted a child
- Bought or sold a home
- Started a business or side job
- Changed jobs or retired
- Started receiving Social Security or taking retirement withdrawals
- Moved to a different city

Some of these mean updating your withholding or estimated payments now, rather than finding out next April.
