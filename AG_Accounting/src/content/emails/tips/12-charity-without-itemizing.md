Starting with the 2026 tax year, people who take the standard deduction can deduct up to $1,000 of cash gifts to qualified charities, or $2,000 for married couples filing jointly.

A few things to know:

- It applies to cash gifts, including checks and card payments, to qualified charities.
- Gifts to individuals don’t count.
- Keep a record of every gift, and get a written acknowledgment for any single gift of $250 or more.

Save this year’s receipts in your tax folder now.
