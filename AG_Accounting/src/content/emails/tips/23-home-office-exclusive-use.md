To deduct a home office, you generally need to use the space regularly and exclusively for your business. A spare room that’s only an office can qualify. A dining table that’s also used for family dinners doesn’t.

The simplified method lets you deduct $5 per square foot, up to 300 square feet, with no depreciation to track. The regular method can be worth more but takes more records.

Employees who work from home generally can’t take this deduction on their federal return; it’s for self-employed people and business owners.
