The Earned Income Tax Credit helps workers with low to moderate income, and because it’s refundable, it can mean money back even if you owe no tax.

For 2026, the maximum credit ranges from $664 for workers without qualifying children to $8,231 for families with three or more.

People sometimes miss it because their income changed, they were self-employed, or they didn’t realize they qualified. Workers without children can qualify too.

You need earned income, and you, your spouse, and any qualifying children generally need valid Social Security numbers. We’ll check your eligibility when we prepare your return.
