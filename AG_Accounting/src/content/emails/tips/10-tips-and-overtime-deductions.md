For 2025 through 2028, the tax law added two deductions for workers:

- **Qualified tips:** up to $25,000 a year for people in jobs where tips are customary
- **Qualified overtime pay:** up to $12,500 a year ($25,000 for joint filers), phasing out above $150,000 of income ($300,000 joint)

You can claim them whether or not you itemize. Both have specific rules about what counts, and they reduce income tax only, not Social Security and Medicare.

Bring pay stubs or year-end statements that show tips and overtime separately.
