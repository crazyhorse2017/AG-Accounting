Scammers pretend to be the IRS all year, but especially at tax time. The real IRS will not:

- Call to demand immediate payment, especially by gift card, prepaid card, or wire transfer
- Threaten to have you arrested
- Ask for card or bank numbers over the phone
- Contact you out of the blue by text, email or social media about a refund

The IRS usually contacts people first by regular mail. If something feels off, hang up and call us or the IRS at a number from IRS.gov.
