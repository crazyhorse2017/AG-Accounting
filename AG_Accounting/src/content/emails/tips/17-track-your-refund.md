You can check the status of your federal refund with the IRS’s [Where’s My Refund?](https://www.irs.gov/refunds) tool. You’ll need your Social Security number, filing status, and exact refund amount. The tool updates once a day, usually overnight, so checking more often won’t help.

For your Ohio refund, use the [Ohio refund status page](https://tax.ohio.gov/individual/refund-status).

Only enter your Social Security number on official government websites, never on a link from an email or text.
