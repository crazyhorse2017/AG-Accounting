Remember, every taxpayer has rights, including the right to be informed, to challenge the IRS’s position, and to retain representation. If a problem isn’t resolved through normal channels, the Taxpayer Advocate Service offers free help.

And if you ever get a letter, a call, or a message you’re unsure about, reply to this email or call our office. We’re glad to help.
