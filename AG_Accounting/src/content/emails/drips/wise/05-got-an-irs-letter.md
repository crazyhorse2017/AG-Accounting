Most IRS letters deal with a specific, fixable issue: a balance due, a change to your refund, or income that doesn’t match your return.

1. Check it’s real: there’s a notice number in the corner, and it matches your IRS Online Account.
2. Read what the IRS changed and the deadline.
3. Compare it with your return.
4. Respond by the deadline, with copies (never originals) of documents.

Responding on time keeps your options, including your right to appeal, open.
