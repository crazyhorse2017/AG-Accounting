Form 4868 gives individuals an automatic six-month extension to file, to October 15. It doesn’t extend the time to pay. Tax owed is still due in April, and interest runs on anything unpaid.

An extension makes sense when you’re waiting on a form or need time to gather records. Estimate what you owe and pay it with the extension.

Ohio and most cities have their own extension rules.
