Thieves get information through phishing emails and texts, fake calls, data breaches, and stolen mail.

Protect yourself:

- Don’t click links in unexpected tax messages; type irs.gov yourself.
- Use strong passwords and two-step login on financial accounts.
- Shred documents with your Social Security number.
- Never email tax documents; use a secure portal.
- File early, before a thief can.
