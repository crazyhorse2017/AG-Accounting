The penalty for not filing is generally ten times the monthly penalty for not paying, so file on time even if you can’t pay.

Then pay what you can and look at the options: a short-term plan of up to 180 days, a monthly installment agreement (many can be set up online), or in some cases an offer in compromise.

Be cautious of companies promising to settle tax debt for “pennies on the dollar.”
