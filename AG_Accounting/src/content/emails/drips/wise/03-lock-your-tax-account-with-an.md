An Identity Protection PIN is a six-digit number that must be on any return filed with your Social Security number, which stops thieves from filing in your name.

Anyone with an SSN or ITIN who can verify their identity can get one, and each person in the household can have their own. You get a new PIN every year.

Never share it except with your trusted tax preparer, and never by email or text.
