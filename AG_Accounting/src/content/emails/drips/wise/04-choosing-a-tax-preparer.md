Every paid preparer must have a PTIN and sign the returns they prepare. Enrolled agents, CPAs and attorneys can represent you before the IRS on any matter.

Be wary of a preparer who won’t sign your return, bases their fee on the size of your refund, wants your refund deposited into their account, or asks you to sign a blank return.

Always review your return before signing, and keep a copy.
