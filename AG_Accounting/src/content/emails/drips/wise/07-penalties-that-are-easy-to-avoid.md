The most common penalties are:

- **Failure to file:** generally 5% of unpaid tax per month, up to 25%
- **Failure to pay:** generally 0.5% per month, up to 25%
- **Underpayment of estimated tax:** when not enough was paid during the year

File on time or extend, pay what you can by April, and keep withholding or estimates on track. If you have a clean history, first-time penalty relief may be available.
