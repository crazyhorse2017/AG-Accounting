The IRS usually contacts people first by regular mail. It won’t:

- Demand immediate payment by gift card, prepaid card, wire transfer or cryptocurrency
- Threaten arrest or deportation
- Ask for card or bank numbers over the phone
- Text or email you out of the blue about a refund

If you get a call or message like that, hang up or delete it. To check your account, go to irs.gov yourself.
