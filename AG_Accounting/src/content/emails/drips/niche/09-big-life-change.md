Life changes often change your taxes:

- **Marriage:** new filing status options and a new W-4 for both spouses
- **Divorce:** filing status, who claims the children, and how property and retirement accounts are divided
- **A new child:** credits, and a Social Security number for the return
- **A move:** a new address for the IRS (Form 8822), and maybe a new city income tax

Updating your withholding right after the change is easier than catching up next April.
