Rental income is taxable, but many costs are deductible: mortgage interest, property taxes, insurance, repairs, management fees, and more.

The key distinction is **repairs** (deductible now) versus **improvements** (depreciated over time). A residential rental building is generally depreciated over 27.5 years, and land is never depreciated.

Keep your purchase papers and a record of every improvement. You’ll need them for depreciation and when you sell.
