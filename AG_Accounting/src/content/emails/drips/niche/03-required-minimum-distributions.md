Traditional IRAs and most workplace plans require yearly withdrawals starting at age 73, or 75 if you were born in 1960 or later.

The amount is generally last December 31’s balance divided by a factor from the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table. Missing one can bring an additional tax of up to 25%.

If you’re 70½ or older and give to charity, a qualified charitable distribution straight from your IRA can count toward your RMD without being taxed.
