All tips are income, cash or card. If you receive $20 or more in tips in a month at a job, report them to your employer.

For 2025 through 2028, people in occupations that customarily receive tips may deduct up to $25,000 of qualified tips. The deduction lowers income tax, not Social Security and Medicare.

Keep a daily tip log. It’s the best way to show what you received.
