Two credits help with college costs:

- **American Opportunity Tax Credit:** up to $2,500 per student for the first four years, with up to $1,000 refundable
- **Lifetime Learning Credit:** up to $2,000 per return, for any year of college or job-skills courses

Both phase out above $80,000 of income ($160,000 joint). 529 plan withdrawals are tax-free for qualified expenses, but you can’t use the same expenses for a credit and a tax-free 529 withdrawal.
