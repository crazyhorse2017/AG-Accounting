Whether your Social Security is taxed depends on your other income. Add half your benefits to your other income, including tax-exempt interest.

- Single filers: under $25,000, none is taxable; up to 85% may be taxable above $34,000.
- Joint filers: under $32,000, none is taxable; up to 85% may be taxable above $44,000.

These thresholds aren’t adjusted for inflation. Ohio doesn’t tax Social Security, and you can ask for federal withholding from benefits with Form W-4V.
