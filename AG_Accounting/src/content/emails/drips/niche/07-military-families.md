Service members have several special tax benefits:

- Combat pay is generally excluded from income.
- Many allowances, including housing and subsistence, aren’t taxable.
- Service in a combat zone extends filing and payment deadlines.
- Active-duty members moving for a permanent change of station can still deduct moving expenses.
- Reservists who travel more than 100 miles for duty may deduct travel costs.

Ohio has its own rules for some military pay, so ask us about your situation.
