Most rideshare, delivery and freelance workers are independent contractors. That means no withholding, self-employment tax on net earnings, and often quarterly estimated payments.

Your income is taxable even if you don’t get a 1099. The good news: you pay tax on profit, so track your business miles, phone costs, platform fees and supplies.

Set aside a share of every payout for taxes, and don’t forget Ohio and city income tax.
