When you work for yourself, you’re generally expected to pay tax during the year through quarterly estimated payments, usually due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15.

You can generally avoid a penalty by paying at least the smaller of 90% of this year’s tax or 100% of last year’s (110% if last year’s AGI was over $150,000).

A simple habit: move a set share of every payment you receive into a separate savings account for taxes, and pay from it each quarter. Ohio and most cities expect estimates too.
