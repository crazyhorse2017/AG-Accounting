Most small businesses start as sole proprietorships. As they grow, owners often ask about an LLC or an S corporation.

- An **LLC** mainly adds liability protection; for taxes, a single-member LLC is usually treated like a sole proprietorship.
- An **S corporation** can lower self-employment tax on profits beyond a reasonable salary, but adds payroll, a separate return, and more paperwork.

The right choice depends on your profit and goals. It’s a conversation worth having before you file anything with the state.
