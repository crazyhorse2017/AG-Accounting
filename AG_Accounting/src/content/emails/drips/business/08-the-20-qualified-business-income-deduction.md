Owners of sole proprietorships, partnerships, and S corporations may deduct up to 20% of their qualified business income, whether or not they itemize. The 2025 tax law made the deduction permanent.

At higher incomes, limits apply based on the type of business, wages paid, and business property. Starting in 2026 there’s also a minimum deduction of $400 for owners with at least $1,000 of qualified business income.

It lowers income tax, but not self-employment tax.
