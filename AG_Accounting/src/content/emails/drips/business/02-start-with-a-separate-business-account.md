The IRS expects you to keep records that show your business income and support your expenses. The easiest way to start: open a separate bank account, and ideally a separate card, just for the business.

When every business dollar goes through one account, your statements do a lot of the recordkeeping for you, and you won’t have to sort personal from business purchases later.

Then save every receipt the day you get it, on paper or as a photo, and match them to your statements once a month.
