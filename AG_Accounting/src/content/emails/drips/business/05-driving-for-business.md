You can deduct the business use of your vehicle in one of two ways:

- **Standard mileage rate:** 72.5 cents a mile for January through June 2026 and 76 cents from July 1, plus business parking and tolls
- **Actual expenses:** the business share of gas, repairs, insurance, and depreciation or lease payments

Either way, keep a log with the date, destination, purpose and miles of each business trip. Commuting from home to your regular workplace doesn’t count.
