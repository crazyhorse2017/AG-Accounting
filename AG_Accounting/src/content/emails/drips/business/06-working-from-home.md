A home office is deductible when part of your home is used regularly and exclusively for business, and it’s your principal place of business or a place where you meet clients.

The simplified method is $5 per square foot, up to 300 square feet, with no depreciation to track. The regular method uses a share of your actual home costs and can be worth more for larger offices.

Measure the space, take a photo, and keep records of how you use it.
