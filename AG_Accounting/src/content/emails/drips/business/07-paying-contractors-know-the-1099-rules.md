If you pay independent contractors, collect a Form W-9 from each one before you pay them.

For payments made after 2025, you file Form 1099-NEC for each contractor you pay $2,000 or more during the year, and send copies to the contractor and the IRS by January 31.

Make sure workers really are contractors. If you control how and when they work, they may be employees, which brings payroll tax responsibilities.
