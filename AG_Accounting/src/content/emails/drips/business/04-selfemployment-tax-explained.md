Employees and employers split Social Security and Medicare taxes. When you work for yourself, you pay both halves: 15.3% on 92.35% of your net earnings, once they reach $400.

Half of it is deductible when figuring your income tax, but the best way to lower it is to make sure every legitimate business expense is recorded, because self-employment tax is based on profit, not on what customers pay you.

Our self-employment tax calculator on the website gives a quick estimate.
