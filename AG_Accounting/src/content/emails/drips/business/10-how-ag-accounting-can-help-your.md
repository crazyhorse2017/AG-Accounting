That’s the end of the small business series. Thanks for reading!

We help small businesses with bookkeeping, payroll, business and self-employment tax returns, estimated taxes, and planning throughout the year.

A few things worth doing before the year ends:

- Make sure your books are up to date through the latest month.
- Check that your estimated payments match this year’s profit.
- Collect a Form W-9 from every contractor you paid.

If any of these emails raised a question, reply to this email or call our office. A short conversation now can prevent a big surprise in April.
