Your filing status decides which tax brackets apply to you and the size of your standard deduction. Your status on December 31 generally applies to the whole year.

The five statuses are single, married filing jointly, married filing separately, head of household, and qualifying surviving spouse.

The one most often missed is **head of household**, for unmarried people who pay more than half the cost of keeping up a home for a qualifying person. For 2026 it brings a $24,150 standard deduction instead of $16,100.

If more than one status fits, you can usually choose the one with the lowest tax.
