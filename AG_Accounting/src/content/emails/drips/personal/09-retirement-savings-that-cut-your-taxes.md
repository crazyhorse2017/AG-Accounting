Saving for retirement can lower your taxes now, later, or both.

2026 limits:

- **401(k), 403(b), 457:** $24,500, plus $8,000 if you’re 50 or older, or $11,250 at ages 60 to 63
- **IRA:** $7,500, or $8,600 if you’re 50 or older

Traditional contributions lower your taxable income today. Roth contributions don’t, but qualified withdrawals later are tax-free. If your employer matches contributions, contributing enough to get the full match is usually the first step.
