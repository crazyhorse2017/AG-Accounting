The tax law passed in 2025 added several deductions for 2025 through 2028, available whether or not you itemize:

- **Seniors 65+:** an extra $6,000 each
- **Qualified tips:** up to $25,000
- **Qualified overtime:** up to $12,500 ($25,000 joint)
- **Interest on a loan for a qualifying new personal vehicle:** up to $10,000

Each has income limits and specific rules. If any might apply to you, keep the records that support them: pay stubs, tip records, or your car loan statement.
