The fastest refund comes from filing electronically and choosing direct deposit. The IRS issues most of those refunds in less than 21 days.

Common causes of delays:

- Names or Social Security numbers that don’t match Social Security records
- Wrong bank account numbers
- Missing forms that lead to an IRS review
- Claims for the Earned Income Tax Credit or Additional Child Tax Credit, which by law can’t be refunded before mid-February

You can track a federal refund with Where’s My Refund? on IRS.gov.
