Withholding is the tax your employer takes from each paycheck. Too little means a bill and possibly a penalty in April. Too much means you’re lending the government money for free.

The IRS Tax Withholding Estimator takes about 15 minutes and suggests changes to your Form W-4. It’s worth running after any big change: a new job, a raise, marriage, a new child, or a side business.

You can give your employer a new W-4 at any time of year.
