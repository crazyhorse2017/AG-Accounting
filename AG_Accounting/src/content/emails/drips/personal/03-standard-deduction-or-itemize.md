You can take the standard deduction or itemize, whichever is larger, but not both. For 2026 the standard deduction is $16,100 for single filers and $32,200 for married couples filing jointly, more if you’re 65 or older.

Itemizing may make sense if your mortgage interest, state and local taxes, charitable gifts, and large medical expenses add up to more than that.

Most people take the standard deduction. Starting with 2026, they can also deduct up to $1,000 of cash gifts to charity ($2,000 joint) without itemizing.
