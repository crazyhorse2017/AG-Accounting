A deduction lowers the income your tax is figured on. A credit lowers the tax itself, dollar for dollar, and some are refundable.

Credits worth knowing for 2026:

- **Child Tax Credit:** up to $2,200 per child under 17
- **Credit for other dependents:** up to $500 for older children and relatives
- **Earned Income Tax Credit:** up to $8,231 for working families
- **Child and dependent care credit:** for care while you work
- **Education credits:** up to $2,500 per student for the first four years of college

Each has its own rules, which we check when we prepare your return.
