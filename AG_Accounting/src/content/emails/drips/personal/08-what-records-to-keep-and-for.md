Keep records that support your return for at least three years after you file. That includes W-2s, 1099s, receipts for deductions, and records of credits you claimed.

Keep some records longer:

- Records about a home or investments: until three years after you report the sale
- Records for a bad debt or worthless securities loss: seven years
- Copies of your returns: many people keep them permanently

Digital copies are fine if they’re complete and readable.
